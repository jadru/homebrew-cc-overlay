# Business Model Validation

CC-Overlay remains free and MIT licensed while the pet-first retention loop and
its supporting guardrails are being validated. Pricing is not a substitute for
evidence that people return for the companion.

## Stage 0 — Current

- Free signed releases and Homebrew distribution.
- Validate companion activation, weekly return, and guardrail trust in a
  consented research cohort.
- Accept voluntary support only after a funding profile is configured.

## Operating model before monetization

Track these inputs monthly, with a named owner and source for every value:

| Driver | Why it matters | Decision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer hours for provider compatibility | Unofficial provider formats create recurring support cost | Freeze non-core features when compatibility work exceeds 30% of monthly build time. |
| Apple signing/notarization and distribution cost | Fixed cost of a trusted macOS release | Publish the annual cost and fund it before promising paid support. |
| Research cohort activation and W4 return | Separates pet value from install curiosity | Do not price-test before a cohort of at least 30 activated users exists. |
| Support requests per active user | Measures whether a one-time license can sustain support | Delay paid launch if support load grows faster than activated users. |
| Channel-specific conversion | Prevents blended launch vanity metrics | Stop a channel after two cohorts fail its pre-declared install-to-activation target. |

## Stage 1 — Pro Test

Open a paid implementation only when all gates hold:

- 500 monthly active installs **and** a consented cohort with 25% week-four
  companion return.
- At least 30 comparable “likely fits” outcomes with a false-safe rate below
  10% before selling predictive value.
- At least 3 of 10 interviewees say they would pay to deepen the companion loop
  or its trusted guardrails—not merely to remove a limitation.
- At least 30% of feedback respondents select a one-time price of $19 or more.

Test a **$29 one-time** license first only after the operating model has a
12-month downside case: paid conversions, payment fees, signing/notarization,
support hours, and provider-compatibility maintenance. Keep live provider
headroom, basic companion progression, basic overlay, and basic alerts free.
Candidate paid value is longer local history, advanced guardrails, custom rules,
widgets, and automation—not provider access itself.

## Stage 2 — Team Discovery

Do not build a cloud dashboard until at least five engineering managers independently request aggregated capacity or policy controls. Treat it as a separate privacy-reviewed product with a separate buyer and data model.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- No subscription for the current local utility.
- No paywall around security fixes, provider compatibility, or basic usage accuracy.
- No account system before a recurring cloud service creates recurring value.
