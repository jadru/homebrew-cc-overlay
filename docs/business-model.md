# Business Model Validation

CC-Overlay remains free and MIT licensed while it validates a local-first capacity-monitoring workflow. Pricing is not a substitute for evidence that a combined system and AI capacity view saves developers time.

## Stage 0 — Current

- Free signed releases and Homebrew distribution.
- Validate pre-run capacity clarity, weekly utility, and trust in local-only system collection with a consented research cohort.
- Accept voluntary support only after a funding profile is configured.

## Operating model before monetization

| Driver | Why it matters | Decision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer hours for provider compatibility | Unofficial provider formats create recurring support cost. | Freeze non-core provider work when it exceeds 30% of monthly build time. |
| Apple signing, notarization, and distribution cost | Trusted macOS delivery has a fixed cost. | Publish and fund the annual cost before promising paid support. |
| Capacity-monitor utility and weekly return | Separates durable workflow value from install curiosity. | Do not price-test before a cohort of at least 30 activated users exists. |
| Support requests per active user | Tests whether a one-time license can sustain support. | Delay a paid launch if support load grows faster than activated users. |

## Stage 1 — Pro test

Open a paid implementation only when all gates hold:

- 500 monthly active installs and 25% week-four return among a consented cohort.
- At least 12 of 15 interviews confirm that the combined system and AI view prevents a separate capacity check.
- At least 3 of 10 interviewees would pay for deeper local history or advanced capacity rules.
- At least 30% of feedback respondents select a one-time price of $19 or more.

Test a $29 one-time license only after a 12-month downside case includes conversions, payment fees, signing, notarization, support hours, and provider-compatibility maintenance. Keep core system monitoring, provider headroom, basic alerts, and accessibility free. Candidate paid value is longer local history, custom rules, widgets, and automation.

## Explicit non-goals

- No subscription for the current local utility.
- No paywall around security fixes, provider compatibility, or basic measurement accuracy.
- No account system before a recurring cloud service creates recurring value.
